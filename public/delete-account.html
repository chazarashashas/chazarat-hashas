Delete your Chazarat Hashas account

Chazarat Hashas · Last updated: September 18, 2026

You can delete your Chazarat Hashas account, and the data saved to it, at any time — either in the app or by asking us by email. An account is optional: if you never created one, nothing about your learning was ever sent to us, and there is nothing to delete.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Chazarat Hashas (the Android app, or chazarashashas.org) and sign in.
  2. Go to My Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Confirm delete.

Your account is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Or ask us by email

If you can't sign in, email chazarashashas@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email, and password or Google link).
  • Your profile: name, username, city and country.
  • Everything synced to your account: learning progress, streak, notes, concepts and game scores.
  • Your chevrusa and chabura memberships, the notes you wrote in them, and the daily summaries you sent a rebbe.

What may remain

  • If you took part in an L'Iluy Nishmat siyum, the name you entered on it, and siyumim you started, may stay visible to the others taking part, so their shared siyum stays whole. Email us if you'd like those removed too.
  • Standard database backups may keep deleted data for a limited time before they, too, are cycled out.
  • Learning saved only on your own device (if you used the app without signing in) is never sent to us; clearing the app's data on that device removes it.
Questions? Contact us at chazarashashas@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
