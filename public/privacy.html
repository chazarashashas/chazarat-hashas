Chazarat Hashas ("the app") is a Torah study app for tracking progress through Mishnah. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it. It applies to the website, the installable web app, and the Android app.

The short version

You can use almost all of the app — reading, tracking your own progress, playing the practice games — without ever creating an account, and nothing about that use is sent anywhere. Creating an account is optional and only exists so your progress can follow you between devices, and so you can learn with a study partner. We don't sell data, we don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking scripts.

What's stored only on your device

If you never sign in, everything the app tracks — your reading position, streak, notes, and game scores — is stored only in your browser's local storage on that one device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data, or switching devices, will lose it, which is exactly why an account exists as an option.

What we collect if you create an account

Creating an account (by email and password, or by continuing with Google) sends the following to our database, hosted by Supabase:

  • Account basics: your email address, and either the password you set (stored in encrypted form we cannot read) or, if you use Google, a token from Google confirming your identity — we never see your Google password.
  • Profile fields: the name, username, city, and country you choose to enter. If you sign in with Google, we use the name Google provides unless you change it.
  • Your learning data: the same progress, notes, streak, and game data described above, now saved to your account so it follows you across devices.
  • Group learning data, only if you use it: if you form a chevrusa or chabura with another person, they can see your name, whether you've learned that day, and any notes or encouragement messages you choose to share inside that group. A rebbe you've joined a class under can see a daily summary of what you completed — never your private notes or concepts.
  • L'Iluy Nishmat dedications: if you dedicate or claim part of a siyum in someone's memory, the name you enter for that dedication is visible to others viewing that siyum.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of outside services to run the app, each only for what's needed to make a specific feature work:

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account data lives on their infrastructure under their own security practices.
  • Google provides the optional "Continue with Google" sign-in. If you use it, Google's own privacy policy governs what Google itself knows about that sign-in.
  • Sefaria, a public Torah-text library, supplies the Hebrew text and any English translations shown in the app. Requests to Sefaria for a specific text do not include anything that identifies you personally.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers — the app has no ads and no advertising trackers.

Who this app is for

Chazarat Hashas is intended for adult learners — post-high-school yeshiva students and alumni — and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and account creation is not intended for anyone under 18.

Your controls

  • Delete your account at any time from My Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and synced learning data. It cannot be undone.
  • Leave a group at any time; doing so removes your submissions from that group's or rebbe's view.
  • Reset specific data (progress, notes, game stats) without deleting the whole account, from the same My Account screen.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the associated data is removed from our active database. Standard backups may retain deleted data for a limited additional period before they, too, are cycled out.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
